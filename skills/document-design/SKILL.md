# Document design

Design a readable document around its audience and purpose. Choose a clear type hierarchy, consistent spacing, a restrained palette with adequate contrast, useful grouping, and tables or lists where they aid comparison. Prefer the user’s existing brand and layout. Avoid decorative clutter, tiny type, and unsupported asset links. Preserve facts, caveats, and content when restyling. Use structured blocks unless the connected tool supports static HTML and the request benefits from custom design; then follow its current static-document guidance. Do not insert scripts, forms, remote trackers, or interactive controls. A designed document does not imply a PDF, slide export, or live application.

Before changing a stored document, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md) and preserve its current revision, access, publication state, and stable URL.
