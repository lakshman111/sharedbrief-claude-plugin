# Seller pitch

Present an agent’s proposed selling approach.

Include property context, evidenced comparable rationale, preparation options, marketing plan, timeline assumptions, supplied fees, and decisions needed.

Do not guarantee a price or sale date or imply a listing agreement is executed.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
