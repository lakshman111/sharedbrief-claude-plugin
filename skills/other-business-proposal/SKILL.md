# Business proposal

Explain a business arrangement for consideration.

Clarify parties, objective, proposed contributions, economics if supplied, responsibilities, dependencies, risks, and next review step.

A proposal is not a signed contract; unknown terms remain open.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
