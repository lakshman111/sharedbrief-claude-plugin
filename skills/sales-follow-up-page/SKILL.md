# Follow-up page

Prepare a buyer-facing recap after a sales interaction.

Capture buyer priorities, agreed decisions, unanswered questions, and next actions with owners and dates only where supplied. Keep suggested actions separate from agreed commitments.

Exclude internal negotiation strategy and avoid presenting tentative pricing or a pilot as approved.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
