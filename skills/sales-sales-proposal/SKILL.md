# Sales proposal

Prepare a buyer-facing offer for a defined opportunity.

Describe the desired outcome, deliverables, scope exclusions, responsibilities, milestones, acceptance criteria, and supplied commercial terms. Include assumptions and open issues explicitly.

Unknown pricing or unapproved dates remain open; do not turn a proposal into an executed agreement.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
