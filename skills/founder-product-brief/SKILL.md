# Product brief

Define a product and the decision behind it.

Describe users, problem, desired behavior, main capabilities, scope exclusions, constraints, success criteria, and open questions. Distinguish decisions from hypotheses.

Do not turn unvalidated assumptions into customer evidence or specify unsupported implementation commitments.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
