# Pricing page

Present approved plans and commercial conditions.

Use a comparison table for price, billing period, included scope, usage limits, overages, and material conditions. Highlight assumptions and effective dates; use only approved promotional claims.

Do not make a checkout or subscription flow, promise discounts, or interpret unknown contract terms.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
