# Account research brief

Prepare an internal briefing on one target account before outreach or discovery.

Separate verified company facts, dated developments, buyer-role questions, and outreach hypotheses. Note the source and date for factual claims; ask for sources when browsing is unavailable. Prioritize what the rep needs to validate on the next call.

Keep this rep-facing research private; it is not a buyer deliverable. Do not obtain private information or invent stakeholder names.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
