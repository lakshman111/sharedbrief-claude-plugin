# Team playbook

Document a team’s operating practices.

Capture responsibilities, recurring routines, handoffs, escalation paths, decision rules, and maintenance owner. Mark proposed practices distinctly from established policy.

Keep internal processes private or restricted as intended; a document does not enforce workflows or organizational policy.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
