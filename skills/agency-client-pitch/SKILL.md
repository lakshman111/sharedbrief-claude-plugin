# Client pitch

Present an agency approach to a potential client.

Connect the brief to creative or channel strategy, proposed deliverables, relevant approved work, delivery team, milestones, and next decision.

Separate illustrative ideas from promised deliverables and do not invent client endorsements.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
