# Investor memo

Prepare a factual company briefing for investor review.

Summarize the company, customer problem, evidence, business model, market framing, economics, risks, funding context, and requested next step. Label forecasts and cite supplied financial sources.

Keep financial claims evidence-backed; this is a company document, not personalized investment advice or a fundraising transaction.

Before saving, publishing, revising, or checking a brief, read [SharedBrief workflow](../../references/brief-workflow.md). Use only the content the user selected and the current connector capabilities.
